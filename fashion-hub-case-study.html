The Creative Vision

Modern fashion goes beyond just fabric cut—it relies heavily on striking graphic elements, bold typography, and consistent digital presentation.

This project unites **Fashion Hub** (the digital storefront) with custom **graphic design merchandise** (t-shirts and ceramic mugs), proving how graphic design assets translate onto physical apparel and lifestyle products.
